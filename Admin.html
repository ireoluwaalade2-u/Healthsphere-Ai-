Security & Audit Logs

The production version should record administrator actions, permission changes, failed login attempts and sensitive-record access in a protected, tamper-resistant audit log.

  • Administrator-only access enforced by the server.
  • Multi-factor authentication where available.
  • Role-based permissions and least-privilege access.
  • Secure storage, encryption, backups and recovery testing.
  • Never expose patient records to public analytics.
